# Technique T0161.005: Action Incorrectly Presented as Taken by Individual or Institution

**Summary**: A claim incorrectly attributes an action as having been taken by an individual or institution.<br><br>This covers both (1) real actions which were incorrectly attributed to an entity (e.g. footage depicting a person taking an action, with a claim which incorrectly identifies the person taking the action), and (2) claims that actions were taken which never actually occurred (e.g. a post which falsely states that a person took an action which they couldn’t have, because they were known to be in another location at the time).<br><br>To make this assertion, analysts will need to (1) identify the individual or institution who actually took the action, or provide evidence that the action wasn’t taken by the attributed entity, or (2) provide evidence that the attributed entity couldn’t have taken the action attributed to them (or at least that there is no available evidence that they did take that action, other than the claim).

**Tactic**: TA06 Develop Content <br><br>**Parent Technique:** T0161 Falsified Content
