# Technique T0160.002: Information is False

**Summary**: This sub-technique applies when information has been demonstrated to be factually incorrect. False information may stem from intentional deception, misinterpretation, or outdated data. <br><br>Indicators include refutation by multiple authoritative experts or visible contradiction by primary source evidence.
